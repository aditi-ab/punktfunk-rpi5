Wire-compatible with 0.44.0, so you can update the host and the apps in either order.

- **Two players on a Linux host no longer get each other's controller input.** Own Steam profiles are retired: each becomes a profile on your desktop and keeps its name, picture and play stats. Own desktop gives a player a Steam and controllers of their own. See Before you update.
- **Linux hosts that are reachable without logging in serve your library and power controls.** After a reboot the console no longer shows a management token error, every profile lists your games with their covers, and clients can sleep, restart and shut down the box while you are logged in.
- **Macs show each frame sooner.** On a ProMotion Mac a fullscreen stream reaches the screen in about 2 ms instead of 18–27 ms. Native on a notched Mac now fills the panel below the notch, about 10 ms faster; the whole panel stays in the list as "This display (over the notch)".
- **iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV take fast streams at full speed again.** A Mac on 5GbE was held near 1.4 Gbit/s, so automatic bitrate kept PyroWave at its floor.
- **Host video gets priority on Wi-Fi again.** Since 0.44.0 a WMM access point queued it with everything else.
- **PyroWave on Android runs faster and sharper.** The phone decodes the next frame while it shows the last, tells the GPU to clock up when decode runs late, and draws a stream at the window's full size instead of scaling up a smaller picture.
- **A management port moved off 47990 stays moved.** Hosts reachable without logging in and Steam Decks used to take 47990 back, which clashed with Sunshine beside them.
- **Android TVs list only real controllers.** A TV remote no longer counts as a controller or turns into a second pad on the host.

## Also fixed

- AMD and Intel Windows hosts stream AV1 in 10-bit SDR when the session asks for it.
- Moonlight's "Host processing latency" includes the encode on Windows hosts, so the number reads higher and true.
- The web console opens Host settings from a link.
- A Linux host's Logs tab no longer fills with Settings portal and missing-Wayland warnings.

## Before you update

- **Linux: Own Steam profiles become shared ones.** At start the host turns each Own Steam profile into one that shares your desktop. Its Steam and saved games stay on the host until you remove the profile with **Also delete … Steam and saved games**. To keep a player's Steam and controllers apart, give them Own desktop, which needs **Reachable without logging in**. The Steam per seat and Pads per seat settings are gone.
